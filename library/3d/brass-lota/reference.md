# Brass lota — test model (law 30), 2026-11-14

## 1 · Reference
- Object: traditional Indian brass lota (water pot), plain lathe-finished, ~550 ml (size "No. 5").
- Silhouette (museum + seller descriptions): round bulbous belly · flat base · flattened shoulder · concave neck ·
  flared everted mouth with a flat rolled rim. Plain modern pieces: smooth, lathe-finished, a little tarnish.
- Defining parts: belly, waist of the neck, flared lip; small turned bead at the base of the neck.
- Material: yellow brass, polished, fine circular lathe rings, uneven tarnish (darker amber), dull finger marks,
  darker inside. Sheet wall ~0.9 mm, rolled rim ~1.6 mm.
- Sources: hunarsetu.com lota No. 5 550 ml (≈ 9 × 9 × 13 cm) · pepperfry 500 ml lota (7 cm base) ·
  Horniman Museum lota (globular, flattened shoulder, concave neck) · LACMA brass lota (flat bottom).
