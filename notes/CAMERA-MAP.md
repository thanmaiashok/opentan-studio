## Genjutsu ("Reality manipulation")
- "Turn one video into many": remix a community video with your characters, locations, products.
- Modes: Motion transfer · Objects swap · Style (Restyle: redraw whole video in a style, motion kept).
- Inputs: reference video (4–30 s) + up to 30 images (characters, products, clothes).
- Right side: Motion Library (All, Lifestyle, Fun, Pets, Music videos, With friends, Sports;
  Trending / Latest), History, Styles.
